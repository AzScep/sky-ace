ABOUT

Sky Ace is an open-world flight simulator. Fly between mission markers scattered across the world and trigger minigames by entering their airspace.

  • ◆ Ring Run — fly through floating rings under time pressure.
  • ◆ Canyon Dash — weave through canyon obstacles at low altitude.
  • ◆ Precision Drop — bomb a marked target from altitude.
  • ◆ Dogfight — outmaneuver and shoot down enemy aces.

Scores are ranked: D / C / B / A / S / SS. Top scores are stored locally.
