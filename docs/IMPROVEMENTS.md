# Sky Ace — Improvement Roadmap

Generated by a multi-agent Higgsfield audit (5 lenses). Build 2.2 already implemented: particle FX (explosions/muzzle/ring bursts/exhaust), billboard clouds, and pilot radio voice.
Below is the remaining backlog, by aspect.

## Visual FX & juice opportunities

- **Particle burst explosion system** (M) — Explosions feel massive with expanding debris cloud, sparks, and smoke puffs instead of flat sphere
- **Screen shake on impact events** (S) — Hit feedback feels visceral; players feel every explosion and cannon hit physically
- **Bullet tracer lines (glowing tails)** (M) — Cannon fire becomes visible and readable; player can see where shots went and adjust aim
- **Muzzle flash burst on cannon fire** (S) — Cannon fire feels punchy and weighty; visual confirmation of shot every time player fires
- **Contrast flash on critical hits (bullseye / splash)** (S) — High-value hits trigger a brief white vignette + desaturate, making rare scores feel earned
- **Bomb drop trail + falling arc visualization** (M) — Bomb trajectory becomes clear before impact; adds dramatic tension to precision drop minigame
- **Engine exhaust contrail trail behind plane in boost** (M) — Visual confirmation of speed state; boost feels powerful with visible afterburner effect

## Atmosphere & environment

- **Dynamic Sky & Time-of-Day Cycle** (M) — Dramatic mood shifts across a 10-min cycle; sunrise/sunset golden-hour glow overhead and on terrain, deepening blues at dusk, reinforces world size and passage of time.
- **Altitude-Responsive Fog & Atmospheric Haze** (S) — Fog density shrinks with altitude gain; at high altitude, crisp visibility and distant terrain pop; descending into valleys creates visual pressure and disorientation cues for low-altitude minigames.
- **Parallax Cloud Layers with Depth-Based Opacity** (M) — Clouds at different heights occlud and reveal based on player altitude; visual depth without performance cost; creates dynamic horizon variation.
- **Terrain-Driven Wind Gusts & Particle Trails** (M) — Visible wind streaks near canyons and mountains enhance terrain features; boost whoosh spawns a short-lived particle ribbon following plane; increases tactile feedback.
- **Water Reflections & Caustics with Scrolling UV Animation** (S) — Water gains depth through animated caustic patterns; scrolling in 2 directions at different speeds creates living, flowing illusion.
- **Distant Landmark Silhouettes & Horizon Skyline** (M) — Adds scale and geography; silhouettes at world edges (mountains, cities) on the horizon give players a sense of landing zones and landmarks to navigate toward.
- **Ground Detail: Crater & River Biomes** (L) — Adds visual variety and landmark anchors; craters and rivers are memorable terrain features that guide navigation and minigame placement.

## Audio & Voice

- **AWACS/Pilot Radio Callouts for Mission Entry/Completion** (M) — Instantly transforms the game into a believable flight-sim with air-traffic-control realism; immersive narrative voice.
- **Audio Ducking: Music Fades Back When SFX Fires** (S) — Polishes the mix drastically; prevents mud when cannon/explosion/chime overlap with music—SFX cut through cleanly.
- **Procedural Wind & Ambient Noise Layer** (M) — Rich environmental texture; altitude/speed react dynamically; no audio assets needed. Subtle but transforms silence into a breathing world.
- **Cannon Fire Feedback: Rate Variation + Shell Casing Drop** (S) — Gunfire feels more visceral and 'real'; avoids repetition fatigue in dogfight mode.
- **Engine Pitch Bend on Boost Engage/Disengage** (S) — Boost acceleration feels more dynamic and responsive; audio-visual sync deepens immersion.
- **Mission Success Fanfare Transition: Abrupt Music Duck + Crossfade** (S) — Victory moment lands with weight; fanfare becomes the hero SFX, not just background music competing for attention.
- **Proximity Sonar Beep for Ring Targets (Ring Run Mode)** (M) — Audio-based navigation feedback; keeps eyes on the horizon rather than HUD. Engaging, gamified-feel.

## UI/UX & menus

- **Interactive HUD Tutorial Overlay on First Play** (M) — New players understand flight controls and HUD layout before flying, reducing confusion and improving onboarding from tutorial-less to guided experience
- **HUD Context Warnings (Stall/Overspeed/Overheat Indicators)** (M) — Prevents cheap deaths from flying too slow, too fast, or diving too low by adding visual danger zones to throttle/speed/altitude displays
- **Smooth Pause Menu Auto-Resume Button with Timer** (S) — Reduces friction when returning from pause by showing a countdown (3...2...1...GO) so players can prepare, making pause feel less jarring
- **Post-Mission Action Menu (Instant Replay / Quick Retry / Next Mission)** (M) — Keeps momentum flowing post-score screen by offering quick paths (retry minigame, jump to next mission) instead of generic CONTINUE button, boosting session engagement
- **Mobile-First Touch Overlay Controls (Joystick-style Zones)** (L) — Makes the game playable on tablets/touch devices where keyboard is unavailable, opening up mobile audience and casual play
- **Leaderboard Global Search & Filter (by Grade / Date Range)** (M) — Long leaderboards become navigable; players can isolate their best runs or track progress over time, increasing replay motivation
- **Minigame Progress Checkpoint Milestones (Toast + HUD Flash)** (S) — Players feel incremental progress during long minigames; especially Ring Run (10 rings) feels more rewarding when hitting 25%/50%/75% landmarks with visual fanfare

## Gameplay feel & content

- **Dynamic Camera Juice — Boost Sway & Turn Banking** (M) — Camera leans into rolls and sways on boost, making high-speed flight feel weightier and more reactive
- **Combo Multiplier & Time Pressure Scoring** (M) — Chain consecutive ring hits or gates to multiply points (1x→3.5x), rewarding aggressive play and making scores feel explosive
- **Adaptive Difficulty — Per-Mode Scaling** (M) — Player grade (SS/S/A) unlocks harder variants (more rings, tighter gates, faster enemies), extending progression
- **Velocity Indicator & Lead Reticle (Dogfight QoL)** (S) — Green crosshair-offset shows where to aim for moving targets, cutting frustration and teaching lead mechanics naturally
- **Mission Seed Rotation — Daily Challenges** (M) — New ring/gate routes each day (seeded by ISO date), encouraging repeat plays to beat daily leaderboard
- **Screen Shake on Weapon Impact & Near-Miss Warnings** (S) — Dogfight feels snappier; visual screen-space distortion on cannon hits and red flash on enemy-lock makes threat clear
- **Control State Feedback — HUD Indicators for Input Authority** (L) — Visual pitch/roll/yaw gauges in HUD show control being applied, making unresponsive moments obvious and teaching stick response
